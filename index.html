Scan, swap, remember

Swapping cards takes three seconds.

  1. 1

    Show your QR

    One person taps Connect and their phone shows a QR code on screen.

  2. 2

    Scan it

    The other person taps scan and points their camera. That's the whole handshake.

  3. 3

    Both agree — done

    Only when you both say yes do the cards swap. Each of you now has the other's full card saved.

Private by default

Everything stays on your phone.

  • Nothing leaves your device. Your card and your contacts live on your phone — not on any company server, because there isn't one.
  • Nothing is tracked or sold. No analytics, no advertising, no data collection.
  • You're in control. Log out and keep everything, or delete your account and wipe it all from the phone.
